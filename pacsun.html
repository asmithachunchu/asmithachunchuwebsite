Redesigned 5+ key product discovery workflows, including filtering, sorting, category navigation, and applied filter management, improving usability and product discoverability through user-centered design.

01

Research Filters

Conducted competitive analysis and reviewed filtering patterns to understand how users compare options, scan categories, and move from browsing to product discovery with less friction.

02

Prototype Features

Redesigned filtering, sorting, category navigation, and applied filter states with clearer groupings, hierarchy, and interaction patterns for easier shopping decisions.

03

Deliver Solution

Presented a cleaner filter experience that improved visual clarity, product discovery, and the path from browsing to confident shopping decisions.

Experience 02 · Pacsun · Internal SaaS Platform

The BackPac Intranet

Redesigned Pacsun's internal employee platform by introducing 15+ user-centered features, including AI-powered knowledge retrieval, department pages, and internal communication, translating user research into a product vision.

01

Map Needs

Identified internal workflow gaps and translated associate needs into department hubs, resource lists, search, communication updates, and support pathways.

02

Revamp Intranet

Incorporated AI-powered knowledge retrieval, department pages, associate search, discount access, and internal communication into a high-fidelity prototype.

03

Present Solution

Framed the revamped company intranet for executive leadership with practical product, operational, and implementation considerations.

Outcome

Clearer shopping filters and a stronger internal platform concept.

The final work separated two Pacsun product spaces: improving customer-facing product discovery for online shoppers and presenting The BackPac Intranet as an internal SaaS platform concept.
