Pacsun

Keep it Filtered

A digital product design project focused on improving Pacsun's website filtering experience so shoppers can narrow large product collections with more clarity and confidence.

RoleProduct Design Intern

TimelineJune 2026 - Present

FocusFiltering, visual clarity, product discovery

ToolsFigma, competitive analysis, A/B testing

Problem

Making product discovery feel faster and easier to scan.

The filtering experience needed clearer hierarchy and stronger discoverability so shoppers could narrow large product collections with less friction and more confidence.

01

Research

Conducted competitive analysis and reviewed filtering patterns to understand how users compare options, scan categories, and move from browsing to product discovery.

02

Prototype

Designed Figma concepts for a revamped filtering system with clearer category groupings, visual hierarchy, and interaction states for easier shopping decisions.

03

Present

Developed a high-fidelity Figma deck that clearly communicates the filtering concept, interaction changes, and shopping-flow improvements.

Outcome

A clearer filter experience for faster product discovery.

The final work improved the clarity of the shopping experience by making filter choices easier to scan, compare, and apply.
