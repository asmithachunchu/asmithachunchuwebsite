Pacsun

Website Filter System

A product design project focused on improving Pacsun's website filtering experience while also exploring a high-fidelity company intranet revamp for clearer internal workflows.

RoleProduct Design Intern

TimelineJune 2026 - Present

FocusFiltering, visual clarity, intranet design

ToolsFigma, competitive analysis, A/B testing

Problem

Making product discovery feel faster and easier to scan.

The filtering experience needed clearer hierarchy and stronger discoverability so shoppers could narrow large product collections with less friction. In parallel, the company intranet concept needed to organize internal workflows in a way that felt practical for technical, financial, and operational review.

01

Research

Conducted competitive analysis and reviewed filtering patterns to understand how users compare options, scan categories, and move from browsing to product discovery.

02

Prototype

Designed Figma concepts for a revamped filtering system with clearer category groupings, visual hierarchy, and interaction states for easier shopping decisions.

03

Present

Developed a high-fidelity intranet prototype and evaluated feasibility across technical, financial, and operational constraints for executive-facing presentation.

Outcome

A clearer filter experience paired with a strategic intranet revamp.

The final work connected customer-facing product discovery with internal product thinking: improving visual clarity on the shopping experience while framing an intranet concept that could support smoother company workflows and stakeholder alignment.
