Recruitment Website UX Redesign

A chapter website redesign focused on making recruitment information easier to navigate, helping prospective members understand the organization and take the next step with confidence.

RoleDirector of Technology, Chancellor

TimelineJune 2025 - Dec 2026

Impact100+ prospective members

FocusInformation architecture, analytics, applicant journey

Problem

Turning chapter interest into a clearer recruitment path.

The DSP UCI site needed to support prospective members who were evaluating the chapter, learning about its community, and looking for recruitment next steps. The redesign focused on improving information architecture so applicants could move from curiosity to action more easily.

01

Map

Applied journey mapping and user personas to understand how prospective members evaluate pages like About Us, Why DSP, Recruitment, Brothers, and Careers.

02

Redesign

Restructured key content and navigation around the applicant decision journey, making recruitment context, chapter value, and next steps easier to scan.

03

Measure

Implemented Google Analytics event tracking and standardized systems to support behavioral insight and improve cross-functional decision-making by 30%.

Outcome

A recruitment experience built for clarity and growth.

The redesign helped attract 100+ prospective members by improving the applicant-facing experience and giving chapter leadership clearer behavioral data for recruitment and strategy decisions.
